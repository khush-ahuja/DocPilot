# Northwind Robotics IT Security Policy (fictional sample)

## Passwords and authentication

Passwords must be at least 14 characters long and must not be reused across company systems. Multi-factor authentication (MFA) is mandatory for email, the code repository, the VPN and the HR portal. Hardware security keys are required for anyone with production access. Passwords are never shared over chat or email; use the company password manager instead.

## Devices

Company laptops must have full-disk encryption enabled and must lock automatically after 5 minutes of inactivity. Operating system security updates must be installed within 7 days of release. Personal devices may access company email only through the managed mobile app, never through a native mail client.

## Data classification

Data is classified into four levels: Public, Internal, Confidential and Restricted. Customer data and source code are Confidential. Payroll data, security keys and unreleased product designs are Restricted. Restricted data may only be stored in approved encrypted systems and must never be copied to personal devices or external AI tools.

## Reporting incidents

Suspected security incidents, including phishing emails, lost devices and accidental data sharing, must be reported to security@northwind.example within 1 hour of discovery. Lost or stolen laptops are remotely wiped. Employees will not be penalised for promptly reporting a mistake.

## Access reviews

Managers review their team's system access every quarter. Access for employees who leave the company is revoked on their last working day.
