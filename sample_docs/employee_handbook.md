# Northwind Robotics Employee Handbook (fictional sample)

## Working hours

Core collaboration hours are 11:00 to 16:00 IST, Monday to Friday. Outside core hours, employees may set their own schedule as long as they work 40 hours per week. Meetings should not be scheduled outside core hours without the attendees' agreement.

## Remote work

Employees may work remotely up to three days per week after completing their 90-day probation period. Fully remote arrangements need written approval from a department head and are reviewed every six months. The company provides a one-time home-office allowance of INR 25,000 for laptop stands, chairs and monitors; receipts must be submitted within 60 days of purchase.

## Leave policy

Full-time employees receive 24 days of paid annual leave per calendar year, accrued at 2 days per month. Up to 8 unused days can be carried over to the next year; any remaining days lapse on 31 March. Employees also receive 12 days of sick leave per year, which cannot be carried over. Sick leave longer than 3 consecutive days requires a medical certificate.

Parental leave is 26 weeks of paid leave for the primary caregiver and 4 weeks for the secondary caregiver. Parental leave can be taken within 12 months of the child's birth or adoption.

## Learning budget

Each employee has an annual learning budget of INR 40,000 for courses, certifications, books and conference tickets. Unused budget does not carry over. Requests above INR 15,000 need manager approval before purchase.

## Performance reviews

Performance reviews happen twice a year, in April and October. Each review includes a self-assessment, a manager assessment and peer feedback from at least two colleagues. Salary revisions are only made after the April review.
